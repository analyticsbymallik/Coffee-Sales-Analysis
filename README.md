#  Coffee Sales Analysis | Excel Dashboard

##  Project Overview

The **Coffee Sales Analysis** project is an interactive Excel-based data analysis and dashboard project designed to analyze coffee shop sales performance across **products, product categories, locations, sizes, months, days, and hours**.

The project focuses on transforming raw sales data into meaningful business insights using **Microsoft Excel and Power Query**. The dashboard provides an easy-to-understand view of sales trends and helps identify high-performing products, locations, categories, and peak sales periods.

The analysis contains **149,116 total transactions**, **214,470 units sold**, and **698,812.33 total revenue**.

---

##  Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze coffee shop sales performance and answer important business questions such as:

* Which products have the highest sales volume?
* Which coffee shop locations contribute the most to overall sales?
* Which product categories have the highest demand?
* What are the best-performing product types?
* Which product sizes are most frequently sold?
* During which hours are sales highest?
* How do sales vary across different days and months?
* Which products and categories may require greater inventory planning?

##  Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Top 10 Products by Quantity

The dashboard identifies the **top 10 products based on quantity sold**.

This analysis helps determine which individual products have the highest customer demand and can support decisions related to:

* Inventory planning
* Product availability
* Stock management
* Product prioritization
* Promotional planning

---

### 2. Location-wise Sales

Sales are analyzed across three coffee shop locations:

* Astoria
* Hell's Kitchen
* Lower Manhattan

The dashboard uses a pie chart to compare the sales contribution of each location.

The distribution is approximately:

* **Lower Manhattan – 34%**
* **Astoria – 33%**
* **Hell's Kitchen – 33%**

This indicates that sales are relatively balanced across the three locations.

---

### 3. Hourly Sales Analysis

The hourly sales chart analyzes how sales change throughout the day.

The dashboard shows stronger sales activity during the earlier hours, followed by a gradual decline later in the day.

This analysis can help businesses understand:

* Peak sales hours
* Customer purchasing patterns
* Staffing requirements
* Inventory requirements
* Potential promotional periods

---

### 4. Product Category Distribution

The dashboard compares sales distribution across major product categories, including:

* Coffee
* Bakery
* Flavours
* Loose Tea
* Packaged Chocolate

The analysis shows that **Coffee is the dominant product category**, followed by Bakery and other categories.

This helps identify the categories contributing most to overall sales volume.

---

### 5. Product Type Analysis

The dashboard provides a detailed comparison of different product types.

This analysis helps identify high-volume products and understand customer purchasing preferences at a more detailed level.

---

### 6. Size-wise Sales

Sales are analyzed based on product size:

* Regular
* Large
* Small
* Not Defined

The dashboard shows that **Regular and Large sizes account for the majority of defined-size sales**.

This analysis helps understand customer size preferences and can support product and inventory planning.
